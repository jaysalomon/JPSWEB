Abstract

What if the secret to safe artificial intelligence wasn't building better cages, but creating minds that genuinely want to help? This paper presents a biological architecture for artificial minds that age, mature, and grow wise. Rather than external rules, we implement the actual hormonal mechanisms that guide ageing organisms toward cooperation and knowledge-sharing. Our framework implements these principles through a hormonal transformation engine within the MeTTA Atomspace architecture. As artificial minds mature, negative emotions like jealousy naturally diminish whilst knowledge-sharing becomes intrinsically more rewarding.
