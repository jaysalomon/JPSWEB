The Stability-Plasticity Dilemma

How do you keep an AI system's worldview consistent without making it blind to new evidence? This isn't just a Prompt Engineering problem—it's a phase transition problem.

  • Too Much Plasticity (Chaos)

    The system flips its entire worldview based on a single prompt. It has no conviction. "Stochastic Parrot."

  • Too Much Stability (Dogma)

    The system refuses to update despite overwhelming evidence. "Model Collapse / Refusal."

The Physics of Belief

We model AI agents as "spins" in an Ising Model. Their state (Belief) is determined by the pressure from peers (Coupling), external evidence (Field), and their own randomness (Temperature).

J (Coupling)

Social trust between agents. High J leads to groupthink; Low J leads to anarchy.

T (Temperature)

Epistemic noise. High T encourages exploration; Low T causes crystallization (decision making).

λ (Criticality)

The control parameter. When λ > 1, the system "decides". This is the mathematical definition of conviction.
